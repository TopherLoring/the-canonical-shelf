The Bible is not one book — it is a shelf of sixty-six, collected over roughly a thousand years, in four or five different kinds of writing. Learn the shelf first, then the groups, then what is actually in them.

The shelf, end to end — click any book

Each segment is one book, sized by how many chapters it has. Gold underline means you have met it in play.

About this tool. Sixty-six books, the Protestant canon. Catholic and Orthodox Bibles include additional books (Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach, Baruch, 1–2 Maccabees and others) placed among the Old Testament writings; they are not covered here.

Authorship and dating are given the way most reference works give them: the traditional attribution first, then the mainstream scholarly view where the two differ. Chapter counts follow standard English editions.
