# v4 content standards

## 69 mastery activities

Every original requirement keeps its stable legacy ID for auditability, but learner-facing presentation is fully rewritten for the integrated v4 course. A shippable mastery activity requires:

1. a learner-facing title that explains why the skill matters;
2. a short deck/dek that orients a beginner;
3. connected teaching copy written specifically for that requirement;
4. a plain-English summary;
5. a purpose-built visual specification where a visual improves understanding;
6. an understanding check chosen for the cognitive task rather than renderer convenience;
7. a hint ladder only where a hint is genuinely useful;
8. explanatory correct and incorrect feedback;
9. preservation or improvement of the original drill capability;
10. the exact legacy ID retained internally for coverage verification.

Coverage remains fixed at:

- Story — 10
- Order — 17
- Groups — 12
- Chronology — 7
- Content — 8
- Themes — 7
- Verses — 8

The learner encounters these as integrated mastery activities inside the 25-unit course, not as seven detached tracks.

## Visual coverage standard

Every guided lesson has an explicit authored instructional visual. Mastery activities also use visuals where the visual carries relationships, sequence, comparison, scale, or structure better than prose alone.

The visual vocabulary includes:

- timeline
- shelf / canon map
- story arc
- flow / argument chain
- comparison board
- relationship network
- theme thread
- verse-context chain
- book profile
- position spectrum
- translation/canon/context stack
- schematic map

Visuals supplement the accessible explanation and must not become the only carrier of essential meaning.

## Topics reference desk

Launch coverage includes doctrine, Christian practice, life questions, difficult/contested questions, and glossary material.

### Doctrine

Trinity; incarnation; Holy Spirit; creation; sin; grace; salvation; atonement; faith and works; providence; free will/predestination; Church; Scripture/inspiration; canon; resurrection; judgment/hell; new creation.

### Christian practice

Prayer; baptism; Communion/Eucharist; worship; confession; forgiveness; generosity; fasting; discernment; spiritual gifts; church membership/community; vocation/work.

### Life questions

Anxiety; grief; depression/mental-health boundaries; suffering; death; relationships; marriage; sex; money; debt; work; purpose; anger; forgiveness; justice; poverty; violence; politics boundaries; medical care; doubt; unanswered prayer.

### Difficult / contested questions

LGBTQ people and relationships; women in ministry; slavery; conquest; antisemitism/Jewish-Christian relations; other religions; hell; biblical inerrancy; creation/evolution; miracles; spiritual evil; end-times systems.

### Glossary

Curriculum vocabulary is harvested and editorially de-duplicated so learners can move between lesson vocabulary and broader Topics material without a second jargon system.

## Expert-answer format

1. **Short answer** — direct, plain English.
2. **What that means** — concise explanation in context.
3. **Important distinction** — common category error or misuse.
4. **Where Christians disagree** — only when disagreement is material.
5. **Biblical evidence** — relevant references without a proof-text wall.
6. **Related topics** — continue exploration.
7. **Course connection** — relevant unit/lesson where useful.

Topics remains a reference layer rather than another scored curriculum.

## Future conversational extension

If a conversational theologian layer is added later, the curated Topics/curriculum corpus should be its retrieval source. A generative model should be required to:

- retrieve before answering;
- identify which claims come from the course's editorial framework versus broader Christian consensus;
- label major disagreements;
- avoid inventing quotations or verse references;
- state uncertainty when source material is insufficient;
- link back to curated entries;
- never expose provider credentials in the static client.
